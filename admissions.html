How to apply

Join Kanjalu Girls in three steps.

Simple, clear and friendly. If you need help at any point, our office is happy to guide you.

  1. 1

    Get the admission form

    Download it below, or collect it from the school office.

  2. 2

    Submit your documents

    Bring the report form and a copy of the birth certificate, together with the completed admission form.

  3. 3

    Visit and meet the team

    Tour the campus, see our labs and farm, and ask us anything.
