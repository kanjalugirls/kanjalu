Our story · Since 1986

A C2 school in the heart of Tigania West, Meru County.

Kanjalu Girls High School is a C2 school in Tigania West, Meru County. Since opening our doors in 1986, we have grown into a proud home of learning where girls are shaped by discipline, determination and faith.

We are a God-centred school sponsored by the Methodist Church of Kenya (MCK), and a proud CEMASTEA centre. Under the leadership of our Principal, Madam Faith Mutahi, Kanjalu has all the facilities needed for the Competency Based Curriculum (CBE), so every girl can find her pathway and thrive.

Facilities

Everything a CBE learner needs, on one campus.

Kanjalu has all the facilities required to deliver the Competency Based Curriculum well.

Well-equipped computer lab

Hands-on computer science and digital skills for every pathway.

Chemistry and Biology labs

Real experiments and real discoveries for our STEM learners.

School farm

A working farm where our agriculture students learn by doing.

Chess and sports

Chess is one of our proud sports, sharpening focus, strategy and patience. Our Sports and Recreation learners train in the open air.

Two school buses

A CEMASTEA centre
