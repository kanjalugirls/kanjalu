About Kanjalu Girls High School

A C2 school in Tigania West, Meru County, founded in 1986 and sponsored by the Methodist Church of Kenya. We offer all three CBE pathways in a God-centred, disciplined learning environment.

Our vision

To provide quality education for the girls through discipline and determination.

Our mission

To be a centre of academic excellence to transform the society.
